A good 10-minute test

  1. Start on Home and orient yourself without instructions.
  2. Open Parenting and confirm or change one decision.
  3. Open Property and work one asset or debt.
  4. Look at Support, then Review.
  5. Use “Start over” when you want a clean copy.

What we want feedback on

  • Can you tell what to do next?
  • Does anything feel crowded or confusing?
  • Do decisions read naturally for a mediator?
  • Where did you hesitate or get lost?
  • What feels unusually good or intuitive?
